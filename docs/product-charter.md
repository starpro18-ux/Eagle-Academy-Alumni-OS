# Product charter

## User

Eagle Academy Alumni Association leadership and committee leads.

## Initial problem

The organization has strong ideas and community energy, but execution is slowed by:

- unclear ownership after meetings
- inconsistent follow-through
- event plans without a standard brief or budget
- objectives that are not connected to measured results
- uncertainty about the latest document or decision
- support requests that arrive without enough context
- leadership work concentrated in too few people

## First vertical slice

A leader can:

1. Connect or upload a meeting source.
2. Review proposed decisions and commitments with citations.
3. Confirm owners and dates.
4. Create an event brief from an approved idea.
5. Define objectives before the event.
6. Record actual outcomes afterward.
7. See what worked, what missed, and what should change.

## Initial screens

- Command center
- Meetings and decisions
- Commitments
- Events and briefs
- Outcomes and debriefs
- Source library
- Review queue
- Connections and permissions
- Audit history

## Non-goals for the first release

- automatic disciplinary decisions
- autonomous member removal
- unsupervised external messaging
- automatic budget approval
- opaque member scoring
- model training on unreviewed organizational data
- importing every historical document before the workflow works

## Success measures

- percentage of action items with a confirmed owner and date
- on-time completion rate
- time to create an approved event brief
- percentage of events with objectives before execution
- percentage of events with a post-event debrief
- recommendation correction rate
- measurable improvement in participation, fundraising, or objective completion
